Smart-home automation, built in

Locks, cleaning and climate — handled.

Most rental software stops at the calendar. Stayeno reaches into the property itself.

Automated smart lock

Every guest gets their own code, active only for their stay.

  • Unique code per stay, active 1 hour before check-in to 2 hours after checkout
  • Every code read back from the lock to confirm it works
  • Changed or cancelled bookings update access automatically
  • Old codes cleared so the lock never fills up

Cleaning workflow

Turnovers on time, without a single text from you.

  • Cleaner notified for every new booking and change
  • 24-hour reminder before each checkout
  • Same-day turnovers flagged with a 12–4 PM window
  • Overlapping bookings caught before they become a problem

Automated thermostat

Comfort for guests. Lower bills for you.

  • Pre-cool or pre-heat 4 hours before arrival
  • Energy-saving vacant mode between stays
  • Freeze protection when the house gets cold
  • Alert when the AC or heat can't keep up
