Under the hood

Automation you can trust with your front door.

Checked, not assumed

Every door code is read back from the lock after it's set. Every removal is confirmed. If something can't be confirmed, you hear about it.

Never twice, never missed

Stayeno remembers what it already did. Messages aren't sent twice, and anything that fails is retried automatically.

Careful with changes

Moved dates and cancellations are double-checked before access changes, so a calendar glitch never locks out a guest who's staying.
