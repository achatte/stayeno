FAQ

Pricing, without the fine print.

Is pricing per property?

Yes. The draft model uses a per-property monthly subscription rather than a percentage of booking revenue.

Can I start with one property?

Yes. Stayeno is intended to work for individual hosts as well as growing property operations.

Are SMS notifications and thermostat automation included?

Yes. SMS notifications and Honeywell thermostat automation are available today and included in the Pro plan.

Can pricing change before launch?

Yes. The dollar amounts on this draft page are placeholders for the launch model and should be confirmed before the public site is finalized.
